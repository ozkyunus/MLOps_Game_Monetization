# Hybrid-Casual Retention Benchmarks (2026)

## Day-1 retention

For hybrid-casual mobile games, the industry median Day-1 retention is
approximately **34%**. Top-quartile iOS titles reach **35-40%**. A D1 below
~25% is generally read as an onboarding or first-session problem rather than
a monetization problem. (GameAnalytics 2026)

## Day-7 retention

Median D7 retention for the genre sits around **17%**, and **15%+** is the
commonly quoted threshold for a "healthy" hybrid-casual game. Games below
10% at D7 typically struggle to recover UA spend regardless of monetization
design. (GameAnalytics 2026; Tap Nation 2026)

## Day-30 retention

D30 lands in the **8-12%** range for healthy hybrid-casual titles, with 10%
a reasonable planning midpoint. Beyond D30, retention decays slowly: D60 is
roughly 6% and D90 roughly 4% for games that held 10% at D30 (extrapolated
curve, consistent with genre reports).

## How retention relates to monetization

Retention is upstream of all monetization metrics: LTV models assume the
user is still present to monetize. A common industry heuristic is that a
1-point improvement in D1 retention compounds into a 2-4% LTV improvement,
because early retention lifts the entire curve. This is why aggressive
interstitial pressure in the first sessions — which raises short-term ad
revenue but cuts D1 — is usually net-negative. (Tap Nation 2026)
