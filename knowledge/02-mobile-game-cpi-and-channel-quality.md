# CPI Benchmarks and Channel Quality (2026)

## Cost per install by platform

For casual/hybrid-casual mobile games, benchmark cost-per-install is roughly
**$4.22 on iOS** and **$2.97 on Android** (Game Growth Advisor 2026). iOS
installs cost more but historically monetize better, so platform-level CPI
should never be judged without the matching LTV.

## Channel engagement quality

Acquisition channels deliver systematically different user quality. Organic
and viral (invite-a-friend) users are the most engaged — they arrived by
intrinsic motivation. Paid social skews lower: Facebook is roughly baseline,
Google Ads slightly below (search intent is mixed), and **TikTok delivers
impulse traffic with the lowest stickiness** in the genre. (Adjust/Liftoff
channel-quality reports)

## Channel revenue quality (LTV multipliers)

Engagement quality and spending quality are related but distinct. Typical
relative LTV by channel, with Organic as 1.0: google-play store browse ~0.95,
Google Ads ~0.90, Facebook Ads ~0.85, and **TikTok Ads ~0.60** — impulse
installs simply spend less. Viral referrals trend ~15% above organic.
Practical consequence: a channel with cheap CPI and low LTV multiplier can
still be net-negative; always evaluate CPI against channel-adjusted LTV.

## Payback framing

A common planning rule is to expect UA payback within a defined window
(e.g. D30/D90). Payback = cumulative per-user revenue crossing CPI. Channels
whose observed ROAS at the window is far below ~1.0 need creative refresh,
bid reduction, or pausing.
